Welcome to Amestx (www.amestx.com), a service provided by Amestx Information Services, Inc. (“Amestx”). Amestx is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we collect from our users through use of our website www.amestx.com, how we use that information, and the steps we take to protect it. By using our website, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

Definitions

  • Personal Information: Data that can be used to identify an individual, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, and company billing details.
  • Non-Personal Information: Data that does not directly identify users, including but not limited to browser type, device type, search queries, CSJ lookup histories, and other anonymous telemetry data.

Information We Collect

We collect information from our users in a variety of ways, including:

  • Information provided by users when they create an account, register for a 7-day trial, or update their profile (Personal Information).
  • Information provided by users when they use our services, such as browsing monthly TxDOT lettings and searching for project CSJs (Non-Personal Information).
  • Information collected through the use of cookies, local browser storage, and performance tracking technologies (Non-Personal Information).

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect from our users to provide and improve our services. This includes using the information to personalize content and experiences, to better understand our users and their preferences, and to improve the overall performance of our website.

We may also use the information for research and analytics purposes, to help us understand how our users engage with our services and to make data-driven decisions about how to improve our services.

We may also use the information to communicate with our users, such as to send them account updates, invoice reminders, or to respond to their inquiries.

? We will not sell or rent any of your personal information to third parties.

Protection of Information

We take rigorous steps to protect the information we collect from our users from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. This includes implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard the information.

Although we take steps to protect our users' information, it is important to remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure. As such, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of any information.

Changes to Our Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, so please review it periodically. We will post any changes to our Privacy Policy on this page, and will indicate at the top of the policy when it was last updated.
